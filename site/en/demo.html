A diagnostic report, on fictitious data

This report was produced by the ControlDOne engine from an invented set of documents: three import files of a fictitious company, with its invoices, declarations and a freight forwarder's invoice. No company, freight forwarder, number or amount is real. The report itself is in French.

What the demo shows

Three fictitious files: two with confirmed differences, one to be checked. The findings are at the “proposed” stage: in the real service, the founder validates them before handing them to you.

Confirmed difference

VAT re-invoiced despite the reverse charge

The freight forwarder's invoice re-invoices EUR 2,356.28 of import VAT; the declaration shows document code 1008 (reverse charge).

Confirmed difference

Service above the rate card

Customs clearance fees invoiced at EUR 95.00; the validated rate card provides for a flat fee of EUR 65.00. Difference: EUR 30.00.

To be checked

Printed total and sum of taxes

The total printed on the declaration differs from the sum of the amounts read; an unread line may explain it. Hence “to be checked”, not “confirmed”.
