FICTITIOUS DATA: DEMO

Demo

A real report, produced by the program, on invented files.

The documents are fictitious: three import files of an invented company, with its invoices, its declarations and a freight forwarder's invoice. No company, freight forwarder, number or amount is real. The report itself comes from the same engine that will check your files. It is in French.

Open the full report (in French) Download the PDF

What the demo shows

Two files with confirmed differences, one consistent file.

The findings are at the "proposed" stage. In the real service, I review and approve them before you receive them. The report also lists two points that are not recoverable amounts: a calculation difference on a declaration and a point to have checked by a professional.

Confirmed difference

VAT re-invoiced despite the reverse charge

The freight forwarder's invoice charges EUR 2,356.28 of import VAT. The declaration shows document code 1008, which indicates a reverse charge.

Confirmed difference

Fee above the rate card

Customs clearance fee invoiced at EUR 95.00, against a flat fee of EUR 65.00 in the validated rate card. Difference: EUR 30.00.

Calculation difference

Printed amount different from the calculation

On one declaration, the printed amount of a tax differs from base × rate by EUR 20.00. The report gives it as a calculation difference for the declarant to explain. It is not an amount of duty to recover.
