Questions

What to know about the price.

How is the commission calculated?

It applies to credit notes received from your freight forwarder and allocated, to the cent, to differences I reported and approved. Allocation follows a fixed rule, described in the method. The base is the amount of the credit notes excluding VAT; refunds and remissions granted by an authority are excluded.

The commission is invoiced monthly, on a summary invoice. You see every credit note taken into account in your space. Period during which a credit note is taken into account: see the terms of sale (in French) [TO BE CONFIRMED].

What is left to pay with an offered diagnostic?

The €390 excl. VAT flat fee is discounted by 100%. The 20% commission on credit notes obtained is still due. The discount requires nothing in return: the agreement to publish anonymised results is separate and optional.

Who writes to the freight forwarder?

You, if you decide to. You receive a statement of differences (values compared, location in the documents, calculation) and a neutral letter template with no legal argument. You adapt it, sign it and send it yourself. I never contact your freight forwarder, never chase them for you and never collect any money on your behalf.

What if the diagnostic finds no difference?

The flat fee is still due: it pays for the check itself. The report lists the checks run, those that could not be run and why. A file that cannot be concluded is never presented as consistent.

Do I need software or an integration?

No. You upload your documents as they are. An API exists if you want to automate uploads.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer, or by card through a payment provider. Payment terms and late penalties: see the terms of sale (in French).
