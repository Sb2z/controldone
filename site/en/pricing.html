Frequently asked questions

How is the commission calculated?

It applies to the amounts of credit notes received from your freight forwarder and allocated, to the cent, to differences reported by ControlDOne and validated. Allocation follows a fixed rule, documented in the method. The credit note register can be viewed in your area. Basis excluding or including VAT, billing period and how long a credit note remains taken into account: see the terms of sale (in French) [TO BE CONFIRMED].

Who sends the credit note requests to the freight forwarder?

You do. The requests are written in your name, in the first person, without any ControlDOne signature. You review them, edit them if needed and decide whether or not to send them. ControlDOne never contacts your freight forwarder.

What happens if the diagnostic finds no difference?

The diagnostic fee remains payable: it pays for the check itself. The report then lists the checks performed, those that could not be performed and why. A file where no conclusion is possible is never presented as compliant.

Do I need software or an integration?

No. You upload your documents as they are. An API exists for those who want to automate uploads.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or by card through our payment provider. Payment terms and late payment penalties: see the terms of sale (in French).
