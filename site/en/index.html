The problem

In a business without a customs department, nobody has time to compare these documents.

For one shipment you receive the supplier's invoice, the declaration lodged by the declarant, the freight forwarder's invoice and sometimes a credit note. Each has its own format. So the check happens at payment time, by eye, when there is time.

Here is what I compare, with the check code as it appears in the report.

  • C3

    VAT re-invoiced although it was reverse-charged

    The freight forwarder's invoice charges import VAT that the declaration shows as reverse-charged.

  • D3

    Fees above your rate card

    File, clearance or disbursement fees invoiced at a price other than the one in the quote or rate card you sent me.

  • C1

    Duties re-invoiced above the amount assessed

    The duties re-invoiced are higher than those shown on the declaration.

  • E · F

    Duplicates and forgotten credit notes

    The same declaration invoiced twice. The same service on two invoices. A credit note announced but never applied.

These are examples of checks, not an observed frequency. I know of no independent measurement of error rates on freight forwarder invoices. The diagnostic measures yours.

The check, line by line

This is how a difference is found, on a fictitious file.

Each line of the freight forwarder's invoice is matched with the corresponding line of the declaration or of your rate card. If both amounts agree within the tolerance, the line is consistent. If not, the difference is calculated and its evidence is cited.

What you receive

A report you can check yourself.

The report is available online, as a PDF and as a spreadsheet. For each difference it gives both values, the document, the page, the text read, the calculation and the tolerance applied.

Confirmed differences and differences to verify have separate totals. I never add them together.

You also receive a statement of differences and a neutral letter template with no legal argument. You adapt it, sign it and send it to your freight forwarder if you decide to. I never write on your behalf.

A difference in your favour (you paid less than the documents say) is reported too. It never goes into the letter.

What I do not do

I do not say whether a duty is right. I say whether two documents agree.

  • No legal, tax or customs advice.
  • No opinion on tariff classification, origin, preference, customs value, a rate or a procedure.
  • No dealings with customs.
  • No contact with your freight forwarder. You send the credit note request yourself, if you decide to.
  • ControlDOne is not an approved e-invoicing platform.

When a point concerns regulations, the report lists it separately, with no amount, and always with the same sentence (quoted in French, as it appears in the report):

« Ce point relève d'une appréciation réglementaire : il est à faire vérifier par un représentant en douane enregistré ou un avocat. ControlDOne ne se prononce pas sur ce point. »

In English: "This point involves a regulatory assessment: it should be checked by a registered customs representative or a lawyer. ControlDOne expresses no opinion on this point."

How it works

Four steps. The last one is yours.

  1. You upload your files

    A batch of past files, uploaded as they are to a secure space opened once the terms are signed: PDFs, scans, declaration exports, e-invoices, your rate card.

  2. The program compares

    It reads the documents, groups them by file and runs fixed checks. The tolerances are written in the report.

  3. I review every finding

    No finding reaches you until I have reviewed and approved it. Every correction is logged.

  4. You decide

    You alone choose whether to write to your freight forwarder. If you obtain a credit note, you tell me: it is matched with the open differences, and the commission applies to its amount excluding VAT.

What changes in 2026 and 2027

Several deadlines add amounts to check on your invoices.

ControlDOne does not handle these obligations for you. It compares what your documents say.

€3 per item

Flat-rate customs duty on low-value consignments (up to €150), from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028. The item is determined by tariff classification, not by quantity.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2026/382; European Commission, DG TAXUD.

1 September 2026

All French businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices. Small and medium-sized businesses and micro-businesses will also have to issue them from 1 September 2027.

Source: impots.gouv.fr (in French), page consulted on 7 October 2026.

30 September 2027

First annual CBAM declaration, for 2026, for the importers concerned. ControlDOne does not prepare this declaration.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2025/2083; timetable published by the competent German authority, DEHSt.

2028 and 2034

The new Union Customs Code (Regulation (EU) 2026/2108) applies in stages. The EU customs data hub becomes mandatory for e-commerce on 1 July 2028, then for all traders on 1 March 2034.

Source: European Commission, EU customs reform.

Questions

What you will want to know before sending me documents.

Where are my documents, and who reads them?

In the European Union [TO BE COMPLETED: hosting provider], encrypted with a key specific to your company. On the ControlDOne side, I am the only person with access, with a second factor, and every access is logged. A language model (Anthropic, United States) may help read difficult documents; you can switch it off.

Will my freight forwarder know I use ControlDOne?

No, unless you tell them. I never contact them. If you write to them, it is with your own letter, signed with your name.

What if a difference is in my favour?

It appears in the report, labelled as such, at the "to verify" level. It never goes into the letter template.

What happens to my data at the end of the contract?

I return it (JSON and PDF), then delete it within 30 days. During the contract, raw files are already deleted 180 days after each file is closed.

Is this legal advice?

No. The report records differences between documents. Any point that involves regulations is referred to a registered customs representative or a lawyer, with no amount.

What if the diagnostic finds nothing?

A file with no difference is a result, and the report shows it as such. The flat fee is still due, because it pays for the check. The report also states which checks could not be run, and why.
