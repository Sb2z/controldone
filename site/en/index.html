The problem

Four documents, three issuers, nobody to reconcile them

For each imported shipment, a small business receives its supplier's invoice, the declaration lodged by the declarant, the freight forwarder's invoice, sometimes a credit note. Duties, import VAT and re-invoiced charges are rarely reconciled line by line: formats differ, and without a customs department nobody has the time to do it.

VAT re-invoiced despite the reverse charge

The freight forwarder's invoice re-invoices import VAT that the declaration shows as reverse-charged.

Services above the quote

Customs clearance fees, disbursement fees or additional lines invoiced above the rate card you accepted.

Disbursements different from the amount assessed

Duties or taxes re-invoiced for an amount higher than the one shown on the declaration.

Duplicates and missing credit notes

Same declaration re-invoiced twice, same service invoiced on two invoices, credit note announced but never applied.

These examples describe types of checks performed, not an observed frequency.

What is compared

Each import file, document against document

Native PDFs, scans, XML or CSV declaration exports, Factur-X, UBL or CII e-invoices, spreadsheets: documents are read, grouped by file, then compared.

Commercial invoice

The reference document: value, currency, quantities, masses, packages, Incoterm.

Customs declaration

H1 or H7 data: amounts assessed, printed base × rate, VAT payment method.

Freight forwarder's invoice

Re-invoiced disbursements against the declaration; services against your rate card or quote.

Credit notes

Link to the original invoice, allocation to open differences, tracking of differences still open.

What you receive

A verifiable report, and ready-made credit note requests

  • An online diagnostic report, as PDF and as a spreadsheet.
  • For each difference: the two values side by side, with the document, the page and the text read, the calculation and the tolerance applied.
  • Two separate amounts: confirmed differences and differences to be checked, never added together.
  • Credit note requests written in your name, which you review, edit and send yourself to your freight forwarder.
  • The list of points to be checked by a professional, without any amount.
  • With the subscription: check of each new file and tracking of credit notes received.
What it is not

A technical audit, not advice

ControlDOne finds factual and contractual differences between documents. It never says whether a duty, a tax, a tariff classification, an origin or a customs value is right under the regulations.

  • No legal, tax or customs advice.
  • No opinion on tariff classification, origin, preference, customs value, a rate or a procedure.
  • No dealings with the customs authorities.
  • No request sent to your freight forwarder in your place: you remain the sender.
  • Not an approved e-invoicing platform.

When a difference touches a regulatory matter, the report shows it separately, without any amount, always with the same sentence (French original, followed by its translation):

« Ce point relève d'une appréciation réglementaire : il est à faire vérifier par un représentant en douane enregistré ou un avocat. ControlDOne ne se prononce pas sur ce point. »

“This point involves a regulatory assessment: it should be checked by a registered customs representative or a lawyer. ControlDOne does not express any opinion on this point.”

A registered customs representative or a lawyer can then examine the point.

How it works

Four steps

  1. You upload

    A batch of past files, in a secure area or through a dedicated address: PDFs, scans, exports, e-invoices, your rate card.

  2. The code compares

    Reading, grouping by file, then deterministic checks. The tolerances applied are displayed.

  3. The founder validates

    Each finding and each credit note request is reviewed by a person before being handed to you.

  4. You send, we track

    You send the credit note requests. Credit notes received are matched against the differences; the commission only applies to what is obtained.

Context 2026-2027

More rules, more lines on invoices

A few public deadlines that add amounts and mentions to reconcile. ControlDOne does not handle these obligations for you; it compares what your documents say.

EUR 3 per item

Flat-rate customs duty on low-value consignments (up to EUR 150), from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028. The item is determined by the tariff classification, not by the quantity.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2026/382; European Commission, DG TAXUD.

1 September 2026

Receiving e-invoices becomes mandatory for all taxable businesses in France, through an approved platform. Issuing becomes mandatory for small and medium-sized businesses on 1 September 2027 at the latest.

Source: impots.gouv.fr.

30 September 2027

First annual CBAM (MACF) declaration, for the year 2026, for the importers concerned. ControlDOne does not prepare this declaration.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2025/2083; timetable published by the German competent authority, DEHSt.

21 September 2027

Announced date of application of most of the new Union Customs Code, adopted in September 2026 and applicable in stages until 2034.

Source: KPMG; European Commission.
