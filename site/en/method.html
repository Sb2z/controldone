Method

How I compare your documents, and where I stop.

ControlDOne reports three kinds of differences: a calculation that does not add up, two documents that do not say the same thing, and a line invoiced at a price other than the one in the rate card you sent me. Any question of how the contract should be read is reported to you separately.

01

A program does the calculations

A language model may help read a difficult document. It does not calculate, compare or write. Comparisons and amounts come from versioned, tested code: the same documents always give the same result. Statements and letter templates are produced from fixed templates.

02

Every difference has its evidence

For each value used, the report cites the document, the page, the text read, the reading method and a confidence level. Both values compared are side by side, with the calculation and the tolerance.

03

I review everything

No finding or statement reaches you without my review. Every correction is logged.

Two levels

A difference is only "confirmed" when nothing else can explain it.

Every condition must be met. The difference is above the certainty threshold. The values were read with enough confidence and found in the document. The documents belong to the same file. And neither a reading error, nor a footer line, nor a credit note already received can explain it.

Otherwise the difference is "to verify", with the reason stated plainly: a figure probably misread (6 and 8 are often confused by character recognition), a reconstructed total, an uncertain link between documents, an uncertain currency, a missing document.

Why so much caution

An unfounded letter to your freight forwarder costs you more than a missed difference. It costs time and credibility, and it harms a relationship you need. So:

  • the confirmed amount and the amount to verify are shown separately and never added together;
  • only confirmed differences go into the statement attached to the letter by default; you can add a difference to verify, marked "to be confirmed";
  • unreadable data gives "cannot be verified", never "consistent";
  • a difference in your favour is reported, but never goes into the letter template.

Out of scope

What ControlDOne never checks.

  • The rate of a duty or tax (only the printed base × rate calculation is redone).
  • The commodity code, origin, a tariff preference, customs value, the customs procedure.
  • Whether the reverse charge or the flat-rate duty on low-value consignments applies.
  • Whether an amendment or a claim to customs is possible.
  • The VAT treatment of a transaction, which is a matter for your accountant.

These topics only appear as referral notes: the values read are quoted, no amount is presented as recoverable, and the note carries this exact sentence (quoted in French, as in the report):

« Ce point relève d'une appréciation réglementaire : il est à faire vérifier par un représentant en douane enregistré ou un avocat. ControlDOne ne se prononce pas sur ce point. »

In English: "This point involves a regulatory assessment: it should be checked by a registered customs representative or a lawyer. ControlDOne expresses no opinion on this point."

Security and GDPR

Your documents are handled as data, never as instructions.

Isolation and encryption

  • Each client is isolated by a single, tested access layer.
  • Files are encrypted at rest with a key specific to each client, and in transit with TLS.
  • A second factor is required for my access; every administrator access is recorded in a chained audit log.
  • Backups are encrypted.

Documents stay data

  • No text read in a document or an e-mail is ever executed as an instruction.
  • The language model has no tools, no network access and no write access.
  • Nothing leaves without human approval.
  • Technical logs contain no document content.

GDPR

  • ControlDOne acts as a processor (article 28 GDPR): a data processing agreement (in French) is signed with each client.
  • Hosting in the European Union [TO BE COMPLETED: hosting provider].
  • Reading by a language model (Anthropic, United States, standard contractual clauses): you are informed and can switch it off; structured and deterministic reading remain available.

Retention and exit

  • Raw files deleted 180 days after the file is closed (adjustable).
  • At the end of the contract: data returned (JSON and PDF), then deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymised price reference: price statistics only, never an error rate attributed to an identifiable freight forwarder. An aggregate is only published if it covers at least 5 clients and 10 files, and you can object.
