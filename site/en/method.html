Method

Compare, calculate, prove. Nothing else.

ControlDOne finds factual differences (a calculation does not add up), documentary differences (two documents do not say the same thing) and contractual differences (an invoiced line falls outside the agreed rate card). Here is how.

01

Deterministic calculation

A language model may help read a document or draft a text. It never calculates or compares. All comparisons and all amounts come from versioned and tested code: the same documents always give the same result.

02

Evidence for each difference

For each value used: the document, the page, the text read, the reading method and a confidence level. The two values compared are shown side by side, with the calculation and the tolerance applied.

03

Human validation

No finding and no credit note request is handed to you without having been reviewed and validated by the founder. Every correction is logged.

Two levels

“Confirmed difference” or “to be checked”

A difference is only classified as confirmed when all conditions are met: difference above the certainty threshold, values read with sufficient confidence and found in the document, documents firmly linked to the same file, no possible explanation by a reading error, a footer line or a credit note already received.

In all other cases, it is to be checked, with the reason stated plainly: digit probably misread (a 6 and an 8 often confused by character recognition), reconstructed total, uncertain link, uncertain currency, missing document…

Why so much caution?

An unfounded credit note request sent to your freight forwarder costs you more than a missed difference: time, credibility, a business relationship. That is why:

  • the confirmed amount and the amount to be checked are shown separately, and never added together;
  • only confirmed differences go into a credit note request by default; you may add a difference to be checked, marked “to be confirmed”;
  • an unreadable value gives “not verifiable”, never “compliant”;
  • a difference in your favour is reported, but never goes into a credit note request.

Out of scope

What ControlDOne never checks

  • The rate of a duty or tax (only the printed base × rate calculation is redone).
  • The commodity code, the origin, a tariff preference, the customs value, the customs procedure.
  • Whether the reverse charge or the flat-rate duty on low-value consignments applies.
  • Whether an amendment or a request to the customs authorities is possible.
  • The VAT treatment of a transaction, referred to your chartered accountant.

These matters only appear as referral notes: values read are cited, no amount is presented as recoverable, and this exact sentence is used (French original, followed by its translation):

« Ce point relève d'une appréciation réglementaire : il est à faire vérifier par un représentant en douane enregistré ou un avocat. ControlDOne ne se prononce pas sur ce point. »

“This point involves a regulatory assessment: it should be checked by a registered customs representative or a lawyer. ControlDOne does not express any opinion on this point.”

Security and GDPR

Your documents remain data, and remain yours

Isolation and encryption

  • Each client is isolated by a single, tested access layer.
  • Files encrypted at rest with a key specific to each client; TLS in transit.
  • Second factor mandatory for the founder; each administrator access is recorded in a chained audit log.
  • Encrypted backups.

Documents = data

  • No text read in a document or an e-mail is executed as an instruction.
  • The language model has no tools, no network access, no write rights.
  • Nothing goes out without human validation.
  • Technical logs without document content.

GDPR

  • ControlDOne acts as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR): a data processing agreement (in French) is signed with each client.
  • Hosting in the European Union [TO BE COMPLETED: name of the hosting provider].
  • If the language model provider processes data outside the Union, you are informed and can disable this reading: structured and deterministic reading remain available.

Retention and exit

  • Raw files purged 180 days after the file is closed (adjustable).
  • At the end of the contract: return of data (JSON and PDF), then deletion within 30 days.
  • Anonymised price reference: an aggregate is only published if it covers at least 5 clients and 10 files; you can object to it.
